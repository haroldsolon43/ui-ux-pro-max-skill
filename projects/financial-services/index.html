Ask us directly
Are you a fiduciary?

Yes — at all times, on every account. We're legally required to act in your best interest, and we accept no commissions or referral payments.

How do you get paid?

Only through the transparent annual fee shown above. We don't earn more by recommending any particular product.

Is there an account minimum?

Our Essentials service starts at $250,000 in investable assets. If you're not there yet, we're happy to point you to good resources.

Where is my money held?

Your assets stay in your name at an independent, qualified custodian. We have authority to manage investments, but never to withdraw funds.

Can I leave at any time?

Yes. There are no lock-ins or exit fees — your accounts remain yours, whether you stay with us or not.
